Research Design

This study adopts a mixed-methods research design with a quantitative-dominant, within-subject (repeated-measures) structure. All participants are exposed to the same two tasks and the same conditions, allowing each participant's responses to serve as their own baseline for comparison.

The study examines how XJTLU students integrate AI tools into academic literature reading and summarization. It combines quantitative measures — Likert-scale ratings of confidence, perceived accuracy, and clarity of understanding (collected both before and after reading the paper), accuracy of AI-assisted answers against the actual paper, time taken to identify each element, number of AI queries used, and the AI service used.

Tasks Given

  1. Task 1: Paper Comprehension

    2. Participants use AI only, without reading the paper, to identify the research question, method, independent and dependent variables, and limitations; they then read the paper and verify their answers. Confidence, perceived accuracy, and clarity are rated before and after reading.

    Link to the research paper used: Task 1 Paper

  2. Task 2: AI-Assisted Summarization

    3. Participants evaluate an AI-generated summary on Likert-scale items before reading the paper, then re-rate confidence, judged accuracy, and clarity after reading, and indicate whether they would ask AI for clearer understanding.

    Link to research paper used: Task 2 Paper

Research Variables

Independent Variables

  • Types of AI tools used
  • Context of AI usage

Outcome Variables

  • Confidence in understanding
  • Perceived accuracy of AI-generated content
  • Clarity of comprehension
