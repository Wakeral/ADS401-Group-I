ADS401 / PRESENTATION 3

AI Literacy and
Academic Integrity Anxiety

Survey results from postgraduate students at XJTLU

GROUP I
11 postgraduate respondents
01  RESEARCH QUESTIONSPEAKER 1

Research question

Postgraduate students at XJTLU

Main relationship

How does AI literacy relate to academic integrity anxiety?

Background differences

Does this relationship differ by discipline and language background?

Study approach

A cross-sectional questionnaire of students' self-reported knowledge, AI use and concerns.

02  SURVEY METHOD AND ANALYSISSPEAKER 2

Survey method and analysis

Tencent Questionnaire in English and Chinese, designed for about 20 minutes

Participants

  • 11 responses
  • 10 master's students
  • 1 doctoral student

Measures

  • AI literacy: Q21–27 mean
  • Overall anxiety: Q46
  • Self-reported ratings

Analysis

  • Counts and item means
  • Spearman correlation
  • Exact permutation test

Scoring

Exploratory 7-item index, not a validated scale. Both scores use 1–5.
All 11 records have complete answers to these items.

04  CONCLUSIONS AND REFLECTIONSPEAKER 4

Conclusions and reflection

The observed negative association does not establish causation

Current finding

Risk awareness was high.
Anxiety was mostly mild.
The association was not significant.

Background differences

Differences by discipline and language background were not tested.
