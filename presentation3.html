Description
One cross-sectional online self-report questionnaire (11 responses), not randomized condition assignments or comprehension-test scores.

Measures
AI-use frequency, confidence/skills, integrity perceptions, perceived comprehension change, and open-text workflow reflections.

WHAT WE LEARNED TO DO NEXT

Run the comparison

Compare the AI-assisted reading condition with reading practice; keep paper difficulty, time, and instructions consistent.

Measure learning directly

Use blinded scoring of no-AI comprehension answers and concept maps; record reading time and NASA-TLX; interview after task.

Experiment procedure

1  Paper screening

Show 3–5 titles and abstracts; use XJTLU AI to select the most relevant paper for a research question. Record time, AI queries, and choice accuracy.

2  Key information

Extract research question, contribution, method/data, findings, and limitations. Record completion time, completeness, and whether full-paper reading is still needed.

3  Terminology

Identify five difficult terms and ask XJTLU AI to explain them. Record time per term and whether the explanation lets the student continue reading.

Plan
Consent → eligibility screen → comparable thesis-relevant paper → random assignment to AI-assisted reading or reading practice → timed reading → no-AI/no-paper comprehension questions + concept map → NASA-TLX → brief interview.
