Research Objectives

Understanding the aims and expected outcomes of our study.

Research Question

How does AI-assisted literature reading and summarization influence students' independent comprehension of academic papers?

Research Objectives

Derived from the survey constructs and the experimental reading tasks, this study is guided by five primary objectives:

  1. Evaluate Confidence Calibration: To measure the gap between students' confidence in AI-generated answers/summaries and the actual accuracy of those outputs after independent verification.
  2. Assess the Impact of Verification: To determine how reading the original text changes students' confidence, perceived accuracy, and clarity of understanding.
  3. Identify Moderating Variables: To investigate whether AI literacy, frequency of tool use, discipline, and language background influence the size of the confidence-accuracy gap.
  4. Explore Academic Integrity Anxiety: To examine how students' anxiety regarding AI-related academic misconduct relates to their AI usage patterns and verification behaviors.
  5. Analyze Workflow Changes: To qualitatively explore how AI assistance alters students' independent comprehension, note-taking, and literature synthesis processes.
